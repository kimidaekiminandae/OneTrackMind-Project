What is One Track Mind

Welcome to One Track Mind, the ultimate dating app for music lovers who want real connections, not endless small talk. 🎶💬
Here, you’re focused on one match at a time — because the best relationships deserve your full attention (and your best playlists).
How it works:
One Track Mind matches you with people who vibe with your music taste. You’ll get a peek at their top 3 favorite songs, artists, and albums, along with their profile pic and how far away they are. If you both swipe right, you unlock even more music insights and can start a live chat to get to know each other better.
The twist?
You can only have one active chat at a time. No distractions, no games — just one real chance to connect. You can end the chat anytime by rejecting it, or "complete" it if you’re ready to swap socials and take things to the next level. Completed or ended chats are tucked away in your archive, with just the match’s name and your last chat date saved.

How to use One Track Mind

Getting started is easy — here’s your guide to finding real connections through music:
👤 1. Set Up Your Profile
Before you dive in, head over to the Profile page. Here, you can:
Choose your nickname and upload a profile photo.
Set your swipe preferences — like age range, distance, and whether you want to match more by favorite songs, artists, or albums.
This helps us show you people who truly match your vibe!
🎵 2. Swipe and Match
Next, jump into the Home page.
You’ll scroll through profiles showing users' top 3 songs, artists, and albums, plus their photo and distance from you. Swipe right if you feel a connection — or swipe left to keep exploring. When you and another user both swipe right, it’s a match! 🎉
💬 3. Start Chatting
Once you’ve matched, head to the Chats page to kick off the conversation. While chatting, you can:
Search songs and artists to see how your music tastes compare.
Build up streaks by keeping the chat alive and earn badges that unlock bonus features (like viewing your match’s public playlists!).
Remember: you can only have one active chat at a time, so make it count!
🛑 4. End or Complete a Chat
At any point during the conversation, either of you can:
Unmatch and complete the chat if it’s not the right fit, or if you’re ready to exchange contact info and take it beyond the app.
Completed chats are archived — you'll still see their name and last chat date in your Chats page.
And that’s it! Set up your profile, swipe, match, and let your shared love of music spark something amazing. 🎧❤️
